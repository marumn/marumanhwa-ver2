Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

At Maru Manhwa, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when using our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly when creating an account, using our website, or contacting us for support.

  • Account Information: Your email address, username, profile information, and authentication data associated with your account.
  • Reading and Usage Data: Your bookmarks, reading history, preferences, reactions, and comments.
  • Device Information: Limited technical information, such as browser type and device information, where collected by our website or its service providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Maru Manhwa.
  • Manage your account and save your reading progress.
  • Personalize your reading experience and preferences.
  • Enable bookmarks, reactions, comments, and other features.
  • Respond to support requests and privacy inquiries.
  • Protect our website against fraud, spam, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Send service-related updates when necessary.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate Maru Manhwa, such as Firebase for authentication, database services, and file storage where enabled.

We may also disclose information when required by law, when necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others, or with your consent.

Information you choose to post publicly, such as comments or a username displayed alongside your activity, may be visible to other website users.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Maru Manhwa and its service providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies when necessary to support website functionality, maintain sessions, remember preferences, or improve the user experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain technologies may affect account login or other website features.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with your account. Authentication and related services may be handled by Firebase.

Passwords are handled through the authentication provider's security mechanisms. Maru Manhwa does not need access to your actual password to operate your account.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

You can update available account information through your account settings. To request assistance with personal information or account deletion, contact us using the email address below.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services, including Firebase, to provide certain features. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

For more information about Google's services, please review the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation .

8. Children's Privacy

Maru Manhwa is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, or legal obligations. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
