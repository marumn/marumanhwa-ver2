DMCA / Copyright Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

Maru Manhwa respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users and contributors to do the same. We take copyright concerns seriously and will review properly submitted notices concerning material made available through our website.

This policy describes a process for submitting copyright complaints and counter-notifications. The United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) may apply to qualifying services and claims. Other applicable copyright laws may also apply.

2. Filing a Copyright Infringement Notice

If you believe that material available on Maru Manhwa infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to represent, please send a written notice to our copyright contact at the email address listed below.

To help us review your notice, please include the following information:

  1. An electronic or physical signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed. If multiple works are involved, provide a representative list where appropriate.
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient to help us locate it, such as the direct URL of the relevant page or chapter.
  4. Information reasonably sufficient to contact you, such as your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

After receiving a notice, we will review the information provided and take appropriate action where warranted and required by applicable law. This may include removing or disabling access to the identified material.

3. Counter-Notification

If you believe that material you submitted was removed or disabled because of a mistake or misidentification, you may send a written counter-notification to our copyright contact.

A counter-notification should include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before removal.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number.
  5. The applicable statements required by law, including the jurisdiction and service-of-process consent where required for a DMCA counter-notice.

We will review valid counter-notifications and handle them in accordance with applicable law. In qualifying DMCA cases, this may involve forwarding the counter-notification to the original complaining party and restoring the material according to the statutory process and applicable deadlines.

Maru Manhwa may restrict or terminate accounts belonging to users who repeatedly infringe intellectual property rights, where appropriate and consistent with applicable law.

We may also remove reported material, restrict access to relevant features, or take other reasonable action in response to substantiated copyright complaints.

6. Misrepresentation

Please ensure that any copyright notice or counter-notification you submit is accurate and made in good faith.

Knowingly making material misrepresentations in a copyright notice or counter-notification may have legal consequences under applicable law.

7. Important Information

Submitting a notice does not automatically establish that infringement has occurred. We may request additional information where necessary to evaluate a complaint.
